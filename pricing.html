How we quote

Four things move a price.

Nothing else does. No seasonal rates, no weekend surcharge, no fee for the card you pay with.

Vehicle size

Packages are priced for a sedan. More surface means more hours — hence the flat +20% on SUVs, trucks and seven-seaters.

Starting condition

A garage-kept car may need one cutting stage; a neglected daily three. The free inspection fixes the stage count before you commit.

Level of protection

Three-month sealant, six-month ceramic or a nine-year coating system — protection is the biggest swing in the quote, and it's your call.

Stages, not hours

Correction is sold by stage, not by the clock. Scope is fixed at booking, so an Essential can't quietly become an Ultimate mid-job.
