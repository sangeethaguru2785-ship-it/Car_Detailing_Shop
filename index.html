How it works

Four steps to a flawless finish.

01

Deep Inspection

We map paint depth panel by panel, log every defect under inspection lighting and agree a finish target with you before a single pad touches paint.

  • Paint gauge readings
  • Defect mapping photos
  • Book Now
02

Decontamination

Foam pre-soak, iron fallout dissolve, tar removal and a mechanical clay bar pass leave the surface chemically and mechanically clean.

  • Iron & tar removal
  • Clay bar decontamination
  • Book Now
03

Correction

Compound, polish and refine in stages until swirls are gone and gloss meters read their highest — never at the expense of clear coat thickness.

  • 2–3 stage machine polish
  • Gloss meter verified
  • Book Now
04

Protect & Cure

Ceramic coating or PPF is applied in a filtered bay, then cured under controlled heat before your handover walkthrough and warranty card.

  • 9H coating or PPF
  • Written warranty
  • Book Now
