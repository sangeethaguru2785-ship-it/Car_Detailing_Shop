Four days in the bay
Day 1

Inspection & decontamination

Paint gauged panel by panel, foam pre-soak, iron dissolve and clay bar. Arrival photos logged before anything else was touched.

Day 2

First cutting stage

A heavy cut on a microfiber pad levels the holograms, then gloss is checked under 6000K light before moving on.

Day 3

Refinement

Two finer polishes remove any haze the cut left behind — panel by panel, with gauge readings after every pass.

Day 4

Coat & handover

IPA wipe, graphene coating, heat-assisted cure, then the walkthrough with the written report and warranty card.

Behind the lens

Shot like we work.

The photos on this page are documentation, not marketing — produced by the same process, on every car, every time.

Same lights as the work

Every photo is taken under the same 6000K inspection lights the car was corrected under — if a defect survived, the camera sees it.

Fixed angles

Three shots per stage from taped floor marks: front three-quarter, rear three-quarter and a close-up of the worst panel.

No editing

White balance matched to the lights, straight out of camera. No filters, no retouching, no cloning out the bit we missed.

Documented each stage
