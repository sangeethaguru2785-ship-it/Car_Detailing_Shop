Our story

We measure before we touch.

STACKLY began in a single rented bay with one polisher and a paint depth gauge. The premise was simple: never guess. Every panel is measured, photographed and logged before work begins, so nothing is removed that shouldn't be — and nothing is left that should.

Today four technicians share a climate-controlled, dust-filtered studio, but the method hasn't changed. Inspection first, chemistry second, patience always.

Inside the studio

A clean room for paint.

Four climate-controlled bays, colour-matched lighting and filtered air — the unglamorous infrastructure that makes a good finish repeatable.

Colour-accurate lighting

5000K, CRI-95 LED arrays run along both sides of every bay, so swirls and holograms show up before the customer ever sees the car.

Filtered, positive air

Ceiling filtration and positive pressure keep airborne fallout off fresh polish and curing coating — the main reason work never happens outside.

Climate control

Bays hold 18–24 °C year round, so coatings cure on schedule and leather, glue and trim behave the way the manufacturer intended.

Spot-free water

Reverse-osmosis and deionised final rinses mean no water spots to chase after a wash — and no towels dragged over mineral crust.

Measurement tooling

Paint depth gauges, gloss meters and IR thermometers live at every bay. Readings are logged per panel and copied into your handover file.

Monitored & secured

Camera coverage, alarm response and key-control mean your car stays in one place, in one condition, from drop-off to collection.

Walled-off wash bay

Wet decontamination runs in its own drained bay with its own foam lance and two-bucket station, so grit and chemicals never travel across the dry polish floor.

Infrared curing

Short-wave IR lamps bring panels to a controlled cure temperature, so ceramic and film set evenly through the night instead of waiting on ambient heat.

Lift & undercarriage access

A two-post lift raises the car to eye level for wheels, arches, sills and underside work — no kneeling on cold concrete to miss half the surface.

Handover inspection bay

The final walk-round happens on a dedicated pad under the same colour-correct light we corrected under, so you see exactly what we saw before the keys change hands.
